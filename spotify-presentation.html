Who actually pays

Listeners — free and Premium — plus advertisers buying access to the free-tier audience. Labels and publishers are suppliers, paid via royalties, not customers.

One subscription unlocks the global catalog. The real differentiator is personalization — Discover Weekly, Wrapped — not the catalog itself.

03 — Margin

Where the money actually goes

Cost of revenue — 66.6%
Operating costs — 19.7%
Operating income — 13.7%

Royalties are paid pro-rata on streamshare — Spotify has limited pricing leverage over its single biggest cost line. Premium cost of revenue specifically has fallen from 68% to 65% of Premium revenue since 2024, driven by price increases, a shift toward higher-margin audiobooks/podcasts, and marketplace programs like Discovery Mode.

The right benchmark isn't SaaS

33.4%Netflix operating margin
13.7%Spotify operating margin

Netflix owns more of its own content and negotiates licensing on its own terms — a ~2.4x margin gap that's structural, not a rounding error.

05 — Funnel

Conversion happens inside the free tier, not outside it

"New Premium Subscribers are sourced primarily from the conversion of Ad-Supported Users to Premium Subscribers."— Spotify, FY2025 20-F

01

Bi-annual free trial campaigns

Named by Spotify as the primary growth driver for new subscribers — a no-risk trial window aimed at existing free users, targeted using their own listening history.

02

Calibrated feature-gating

Ads, no offline, lower quality — but a friction dial, not a one-way ratchet. An 18-month test found loosening the shuffle restriction improved both retention and conversion.

03

Family / Duo / Student pricing

Price discrimination by household size and life stage — officially credited with improving both conversion and retention.

08
06 — Retention

Wrapped: the biggest media moment Spotify never buys

200M

engaged users in 24 hours (2025) — its fastest ramp ever; 2024 took 62 hours to reach the same mark.

500M

shares in 24 hours — each one a zero-CAC impression in front of the sharer's own network.

Personalization (it's about you) + social currency (your taste is your identity) + annual scarcity (once a year, then gone) — stacked on data Spotify already has, at near-zero marginal cost.

Spotify does not disclose a churn rate. The link between Wrapped and reduced churn is a logical mechanism from timing and engagement data — not an independently measured fact.

What We'd Learn

Four ideas worth stealing

01

Make the free tier the acquisition channel, not a side motion

Instrument conversion inside the product, not around it with ads.

02

Let data reduce friction, don't just add it

Spotify's own test found removing a restriction grew both retention and conversion.

03

Turn your usage data into a shareable ritual

500M organic shares in 24 hours, at near-zero marginal cost.

04

Know whether your margin ceiling is structural or operational

Before trying to fix it with the wrong lever.
